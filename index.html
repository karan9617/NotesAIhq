Help & Support

Find answers to frequently asked questions

How do I get started with NotesAI?
Simply sign up for a free account, and you'll be guided through a quick tutorial. You can start taking notes immediately using text, voice, or by uploading documents.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely! We use end-to-end encryption and store your data on secure servers. Your privacy is our top priority, and we never share your information with third parties.
Can I use NotesAI offline?
Yes! Our mobile and desktop apps support offline mode. Your notes will automatically sync when you reconnect to the internet.
What languages are supported?
NotesAI supports over 50 languages for both text and voice input, with AI features available in all major languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, and Japanese.
How can I contact support?
You can reach our support team 24/7 through the in-app chat, email us at support@notesai.com, or visit our comprehensive help center for detailed guides and tutorials.
